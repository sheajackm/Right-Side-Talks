Who We Are

The Voice of Young
Conservative America

Right Side Talks LLC is a national youth conservative media organization built by young Americans, for young Americans. We believe the next generation deserves a platform that reflects their values—freedom, faith, family, and country.

We create bold, unapologetic content that cuts through the noise of mainstream media and delivers the perspective that too often goes unheard on campuses and in communities across the country.

Whether you're a first-time voter or a lifelong patriot, Right Side Talks is your home for authentic conservative media made by people just like you.

Join Our Movement

Freedom of Speech

We champion the First Amendment and believe every American has the right to speak their truth without fear of censorship or cancel culture.

America First

We stand proudly for American exceptionalism, our Constitution, and the principles that have made the United States the greatest nation on Earth.

Youth-Driven

Our content is created by young conservatives who understand the challenges and opportunities facing their generation firsthand.

National Reach

From coast to coast, we connect conservative young Americans and amplify their voices in the national conversation.

The People Behind the Mic

Meet the Team

The voices, writers, and producers driving the Right Side Talks movement from coast to coast.

Jack Shea
President & Co-Founder

Jack Shea is an entrepreneur and organizer from Jamestown, New York, and a Co-Founder and the President of Right Side Talks. A 2026 graduate of St. Bonaventure University with a degree in Business Management, he previously served as President of the SBU College Republicans, Executive Director of the New York State Federation of College Republicans, and Vice President of SBU's Turning Point USA chapter, where he also served as Campus Coordinator. He helped build Right Side Talks from a single show into a national organization of hosts, columnists, and contributors stretching from New York to California. RST is driven by the conviction that young conservatives shouldn't have to wait their turn to be heard.

Nick Cribbet
Vice President & Co-Founder

Nick is a St. Bonaventure University undergraduate student, currently studying to be an aerospace engineer. Nick is engaged in multiple startups, including political media and biotech, as well as leading a personal fitness-related social media account. In terms of political aspirations, Nick is focused on moving this nation and the world away from corruption and toward a better future for all Americans.

Cecelia Brown
Creative Director, Executive Producer & Co-Founder

Cecelia is an undergraduate student at St. Bonaventure University from Upstate New York, on track to graduate in May 2027 with a degree in Psychology and a minor in Political Science. Always deeply engaged in the political space, Cecelia is driven by one core belief: doing what is in the best interest of American citizens.

Caleb Grubb
Strategic Advisor

Caleb is a young Christian conservative from Upstate New York with a passion for bringing restoration to the American political system. A grassroots activist, he serves as President of the TPUSA Broome Tioga Activism Hub and hosts the Caleb Grubb Show.

Maura London
Social Media Director & Contributor

Maura is a media professional, broadcaster, and committed biblical Christian. She is the creator and host of the Say It Anyway Podcast, a faith-centered show exploring Christianity, culture, personal testimony, and current events. Professionally, she serves as a News Producer and On-Air Reporter for iHeartRadio, with a background spanning digital media, video production, and leadership through Turning Point USA. Maura is passionate about using media to communicate truth and make a lasting impact through journalism, ministry, and public engagement.

Ryan McCombs
Columnist & Contributor

Ryan McCombs currently serves as President of both the College Republicans and TPUSA at St. Bonaventure University, where he studies political science. He's also a campus coordinator for TPUSA and the volunteer coordinator for Virginia McIntyre's congressional campaign in New York's 25th congressional district. As an unwavering conservative and an aspiring public defender, Ryan believes in free markets, a strong national defense, and a restorative system of justice grounded in constitutional principles and religion.

Austin DeLorme
Columnist

Austin is a Political Science major and Journalism minor at the University of Rochester. He has served as an opinion columnist for the university's Campus Times, covering political polarization, international affairs, and community politics. Austin has also made appearances on Rochester news radio discussing youth political engagement within the Republican Party.

Nicholas Bausch
Columnist

Nicholas is from Syracuse, New York, and graduated from the University at Buffalo in 2024 with a degree in Political Science. He has worked for a variety of non-profits and campaigns across the country, and has collaborated closely with Turning Point USA. Nicholas has been actively involved in the conservative movement since 2020.

Owen Tuori
Columnist

Owen is an Accounting major and Legal Studies minor at St. John Fisher University in his hometown of Rochester, New York, where he is active in student government and business school leadership. A proud member of the Conservative Party, he blends libertarian leanings with a traditional conservative outlook—championing free markets, personal liberty, limited government, and a strong national defense. With a deep interest in how economics, politics, and philosophy intersect to shape American society, his focus runs to the internal workings of parties and how polling and finances move races. As a columnist, Owen looks forward to writing essays exploring the philosophical underpinnings of conservative political thought.

Mike Xu
Contributor

Mike Xu is from Akron, Ohio, and attends Ohio State University, where he's joining the Turning Point USA cause for open debate and reasoning. Once set on becoming a lawyer, he now channels a blend of libertarian, conservative, and Christian convictions into an unapologetic case for American patriotism, sharing his views on Instagram as @Theskilledsamurai. A scholar at heart, Mike is devoted to constant research and rooting out fraud in the legal system, believing a healthy government reflects a healthy society. He champions the American dream and the nuclear family — and says he'll see you in the 2044 elections.

Kobe Kirschner
Columnist & Contributor

Kobe Kirschner is a proud tenth-generation American and a direct descendant of Founding Fathers Benjamin Rush and Matthew Thornton. Born and raised in South Florida, he earned both his Bachelor's degree in Economics and his Master's degree in Business Analytics from FAU by the age of 21. As a county leader of the Fishback for Florida Governor campaign, Kobe is a dedicated grassroots activist committed to making America more affordable and advocating for foreign policy that minimizes unnecessary entanglements overseas.

Jack Molaison
Columnist & Contributor

Jack Molaison is an 18-year-old political journalist and analyst. He started in grassroots campaign politics, speaking at two stops on Turning Point Action's SuperChase Rally Tour, before advancing to lead the youth branch of a Midwestern 501(c)(3). His experience spans campaigning, fundraising, and legislating, and he has been honored to speak at a Wisconsin Forum banquet and at Turning Point USA's CLS 2026.

Joelle Webb
Columnist

Joelle Webb is a legal researcher and policy writer focused on constitutional law, civil rights, higher education, and the First Amendment. She earned her bachelor's degree in Law, Politics, and Society from Drake University and is pursuing a Master of Legal Studies at the University of Denver. Her work has been published on legal and public policy issues, and her experience spans legal research, government affairs, and strategic communications.

Aubree Gearhart
Content Creator

Aubree Gearhart is a 21-year-old senior studying Criminal Justice/Pre-Law, Communications, and Forensic Science. She leads two Turning Point USA chapters — one on her college campus and a newly established community chapter. A lifelong conservative drawn to the movement's spirit of open, like-minded fellowship, Aubree plans to commission into the U.S. Air Force after graduation, following in the footsteps of her father and carrying a deep respect for those who serve.

Ashley Stuart
Columnist

Ashley Stuart is a young political leader from California whose passion for politics began at age eight. She has been involved with Turning Point USA for nearly six years, founding the first TPUSA chapter in her high school district and serving as its president, growing it into one of the largest clubs on campus. She is now launching a new TPUSA chapter at her college while remaining active in local politics. The youngest graduate of the Leadership Institute's CLC Advanced School of Politics and the top graduate of its Youth Leadership School, Ashley has built experience in grassroots organizing, campaign strategy, political advocacy, and event planning through TPUSA and the Los Angeles County Lincoln Club. She is committed to advancing civic engagement and conservative leadership at the local, state, and national levels.

Noah Miller
Content Creator

Noah Miller is a dedicated Christian whose faith and conservative values guide his life with integrity, compassion, and purpose. He values family deeply and considers the support of loved ones one of life's greatest blessings. In his free time, he enjoys football and admires the teamwork, discipline, and perseverance the sport reflects. Noah is known for his strong character, unwavering faith, and commitment to the people and principles that matter most.

Jenna Smith
Columnist

Jenna Smith is a senior at Reinhardt University majoring in Business with a minor in Communications. She plays for the Reinhardt women's volleyball team and serves as President of Turning Point USA on campus. Deeply passionate about reaching America's youth and sharing conservative ideas, Jenna is driven by a desire to help people see things through a lens of common sense and change their worldview with logic.

Halle Janik
Columnist

Halle Janik is a junior in the nursing program at Reinhardt University. She is on the school volleyball team and serves as TPUSA Vice President. Halle enjoys staying active, being outdoors, and spending time with friends. She is passionate about her faith and fighting for this country that God has blessed us with, and enjoys voicing her opinion on politics and the current events affecting us.
